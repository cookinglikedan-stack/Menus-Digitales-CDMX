history_edu The Professional Journey

With a solid foundation as a Licentiate in Gastronomy, Daniel Sandoval has spent years mastering the intricate rhythms of professional kitchens in Mexico City. His expertise isn't just limited to the heat of the stove; it encompasses the management, aesthetics, and logistical flow that define a world-class culinary experience.

Recognizing a profound shift in consumer behavior, Daniel identified a critical gap: many of the city's finest artisanal establishments lacked the digital infrastructure to communicate their excellence effectively. This realization birthed MenuCDMX.

"I believe that a menu is more than a list of prices; it's the first invitation into a chef's world. Digitizing that experience should enhance the artisanal feel, not detract from it."
